About us

A family embroidery house, run on our own machines

The Embroidery House is based in Faisalabad, the heart of Pakistan's textile trade. Every piece we make is embroidered in-house — we don't resell ready stock, we build each order around the design a customer brings us.

That means no two pieces are quite the same, and every order is made to be exactly what you asked for.
